What you can do with it

#office

See who's in today

Your team joins #office when they arrive and leaves when they go. Type /who in the channel to see who's around. Ideal for remote-friendly teams.

#ces-booth

Find your team at big events

At a large, multi-venue event, give each place its own channel and always reach the people who are actually there.

#random

Leave the noise at work

Fun but busy channels leave your phone when you leave work, and come back when you return.
